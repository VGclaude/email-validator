Address Check

Paste an email address and see right away whether it can be real. Paste several at once, one per line, and each gets its own result.

The three above are examples. Paste over them.

What this checks. Spelling rules from the email standard, common typos of big providers, throwaway-inbox services, Gmail's own username rules, and whether the domain exists and has mail servers. What it cannot check. Whether that particular mailbox exists, or whether the domain accepts every address (a catch-all). That needs a direct connection to the mail server, which a web page cannot make. Only the domain name, never the part before the @, is sent to Cloudflare's public DNS to look up mail servers.
